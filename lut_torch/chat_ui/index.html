A next-token model, not an assistant

These checkpoints were trained on WikiText (2 or 103) to predict the next token — a whole word on the word-vocabulary runs, a GPT-2 BPE piece on the others. They have no notion of a question, an instruction or a turn — they continue text. Whatever you write becomes the prefix, and the reply is the continuation the model considers likely.

The context window is small (32 tokens on these runs) and there is no KV cache, so each generated token is a fresh forward pass over the last 32. Everything before that has genuinely fallen out of view.
